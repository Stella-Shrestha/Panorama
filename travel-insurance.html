Protocol & Protection

Travel Insurance & Safety Protocols

Navigating the high Himalayas requires more than physical preparation. We help you understand the protection expected for Nepal's terrain and remote logistics.

A strong insurance plan should include rescue, injury, medical, evacuation, cancellation, baggage and theft protection. Keep a printed copy in your day pack and a digital copy available offline.

Preparation Checklist

  • Confirm trekking and altitude coverage in writing.
  • Share your policy details with your emergency contact.
  • Carry insurer contact numbers and claim forms offline.

The Foundation of Your Expedition

In the unforgiving environment of the Himalayas, insurance is a critical component of your equipment list. We recommend specialized coverage for all trekkers and climbers.

Medical & Injury

Coverage for immediate medical expenses in Nepal, including treatment at clinics and specialist trauma care.

Theft & Loss

Standard protection for high-value trekking gear, electronics and personal documents while in transit or at base camps.

Cancellation Protection

Himalayan weather and logistics are unpredictable. Protect your investment against unforeseen interruptions.

High Altitude Spec

Ensure your policy explicitly covers activities above 4,000m. Many standard policies exclude technical trekking and climbing.

Mandatory Requirement

Helicopter Evacuation Insurance

High-altitude sickness can progress rapidly. In remote Himalayan regions, helicopter evacuation is often the only viable method for rapid descent and life-saving intervention.

  • Coverage for emergency lifts from 5,000m+ to urban medical centers.
  • Direct admission and treatment in Kathmandu or Pokhara elite hospitals.
  • Coordination with Global Rescue or similar 24/7 emergency dispatchers.
