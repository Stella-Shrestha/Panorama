About Nepal

A small country with extraordinary diversity

Welcome to Nepal, the ultimate destination for trekkers, mountaineers and cultural explorers. Located between India and China, Nepal is a small country with immense natural beauty and towering Himalayan peaks.

Nepal is home to eight of the fourteen highest mountains on Earth, making it a paradise for trekkers and climbers. From Everest Base Camp to Annapurna Circuit, Upper Mustang, Manaslu and remote eastern trails, the country offers journeys for every landscape and experience level.

But Nepal is not only about mountains. It is also a land of rich traditions and spiritual depth, with temples, monasteries, festivals, villages and everyday life woven into each journey.

Whether you are a first-time trekker, experienced mountaineer, cultural traveler or family explorer, Nepal offers a journey that can be adapted to your experience, time and interests.

When to Visit

Best time to travel in Nepal

Nepal can be visited throughout the year, but the best season depends on region, activity and altitude.

Spring

Recommended

March to May

Clear mountain mornings, warmer weather and blooming rhododendron forests.

Autumn

Best Season

September to November

Stable weather, excellent visibility and ideal high-altitude trekking conditions.

Winter

December to February

Cold conditions at altitude, but peaceful trails and clear views on lower routes.

Monsoon

June to August

Green landscapes and fewer visitors, with good options in rain-shadow areas.
