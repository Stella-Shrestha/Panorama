About This Journey

Trip Overview

The Three Passes Trek is a legendary high-altitude circuit in the Everest (Khumbu) region of Nepal, designed for experienced and adventurous trekkers who want to go beyond the standard Everest Base Camp trail.

This trek offers a full immersion into the Himalayan wilderness, combining epic mountain views, cultural encounters with the Sherpa people, and challenging yet rewarding mountain passes that connect the main valleys of the Khumbu.

Why Choose the Three Passes Trek?

  • Most complete Everest Region trek - covers all major highlights.
  • Less crowded than the classic Everest Base Camp trail after Namche.
  • Perfect for adventure seekers looking for a real challenge and unforgettable alpine beauty.

Why This Trek Is Special

Trek Highlights

Kongma La Pass (5,535 m) – The highest of the three, offering raw and remote landscapes with views of Makalu and Lhotse.

Cho La Pass (5,420 m) – A dramatic traverse across glaciers, linking Lobuche and Gokyo.

Renjo La Pass (5,360 m) – One of the most scenic passes with a breathtaking view over the Gokyo Lakes and Mt. Everest.

Everest Base Camp (5,364 m) – The iconic destination at the foot of the world’s highest peak.

Kala Patthar (5,545 m) – The best viewpoint to see Mount Everest up close.

Gokyo Valley & Lakes – A magical series of turquoise glacial lakes and panoramic views from Gokyo Ri (5,360 m).

Authentic Sherpa Culture – Explore traditional villages like Namche Bazaar, Thame, Dingboche, and Chhukhung, and visit ancient monasteries.

Wildlife & Nature – Cross paths with yaks, blue sheep, and Himalayan birds in Sagarmatha National Park (UNESCO site).
