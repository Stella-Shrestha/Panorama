Land of the Himalayas, culture and adventure

Welcome to Nepal, the ultimate destination for trekkers, mountaineers and cultural explorers. Located between India and China, Nepal is a small country with immense natural beauty, diverse cultures and towering Himalayan peaks, including the world's highest mountain, Mount Everest.

Nepal is home to 8 of the 14 highest mountains on Earth, making it a paradise for trekkers and climbers. From the iconic Everest Base Camp Trek to the stunning Annapurna Circuit, and from the remote trails of Upper Mustang to the wild beauty of Manaslu and Kanchenjunga, Nepal offers endless trekking adventures for every level of hiker.

But Nepal is not only about mountains. It is also a land of rich traditions and spiritual depth. With more than 125 ethnic groups and 120 spoken languages, Nepal's cultural mosaic is as impressive as its landscapes. Explore ancient Buddhist monasteries in the high Himalayas, colorful Hindu temples in Kathmandu Valley and vibrant local festivals like Dashain, Tihar and Lhosar.

At Panorama Himalaya Trekking, we specialize in creating authentic and responsible trekking experiences across Nepal. Our local expert guides and personalized itineraries help make your journey safe, culturally enriching and deeply connected to the land and people.

Come discover the magic of Nepal with Panorama Himalaya Trekking, where every step is a story and every mountain feels sacred.
