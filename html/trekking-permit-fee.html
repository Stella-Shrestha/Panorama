A separate royalty structure for an iconic peak

Ama Dablam uses a dedicated seasonal permit schedule for foreign climbers rather than the standard altitude-category fee.

  • Spring and autumn royalty fee: $400 per climber.
  • Winter and summer royalty fee: $200 per climber.
  • Peak elevation and technical category: 6,812 metres.
