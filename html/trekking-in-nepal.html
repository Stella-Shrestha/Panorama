More than a walk through the mountains

Trekking in Nepal is a journey through some of the world's most dramatic landscapes. Trails pass through forests, river valleys, high-altitude deserts, glaciers and villages beneath the highest mountains on Earth.

Nepal offers trekking experiences for beginners, families, experienced hikers and mountaineers. You can choose a short trek near Kathmandu, a classic Everest or Annapurna route, or a remote expedition through Manaslu, Dolpo, Makalu or Kanchenjunga.

Trekking also creates opportunities to meet local communities, stay in traditional tea houses, visit Buddhist monasteries and experience the diverse cultures of the Himalayan region.

"A journey designed around you: every itinerary can be adjusted to your pace, interests, travel dates and experience level."

Choose the right season for your route

Trekking conditions vary by altitude and region. Spring and autumn are most popular, while some destinations can also be explored in winter or monsoon.

Spring

March to May

Warmer temperatures and blooming forests.

Autumn

September to November

Stable weather and excellent visibility.

Winter

December to February

Quiet trails on lower routes.

Monsoon

June to August

Good for rain-shadow areas.
