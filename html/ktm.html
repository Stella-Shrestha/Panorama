Experience the Heart of Kathmandu

Kathmandu is a fascinating blend of ancient traditions, magnificent architecture and modern city life. This carefully planned day tour takes you to some of the valley's most important cultural and religious landmarks.

Accompanied by an experienced local guide, you will explore historic squares, sacred temples and remarkable Buddhist monuments while learning about Nepal's rich cultural heritage.

Kathmandu Heritage Tour Itinerary

A carefully timed, step-by-step cultural journey through Kathmandu Valley's most iconic monuments.

08:30 AM Step 01

Hotel Pickup & Briefing

Your private air-conditioned vehicle and licensed local guide will pick you up directly from your hotel lobby in Kathmandu.

Includes comfortable private transfer
09:00 AM Step 02

Kathmandu Durbar Square

Begin your cultural exploration at the UNESCO World Heritage royal palace complex. Admire ancient Malla dynasty architecture, pagoda-style temples, and visit the temple of the Living Goddess Kumari.

Kumari Ghar Hanuman Dhoka Palace
11:30 AM Step 03

Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple)

Ascend to the ancient hilltop stupa overlooking the entire Kathmandu Valley. Experience the peaceful Buddhist prayer wheels and panoramic valley views.

Panoramic views of Kathmandu Valley
01:00 PM Step 04

Traditional Nepalese Lunch Break

Relax at a handpicked local restaurant to savor authentic Nepali Dal Bhat or traditional Newari cuisine prepared fresh.

Vegetarian & hygienic dining options available
02:30 PM Step 05

Pashupatinath Temple

Visit Nepal's most sacred Hindu temple complex situated along the holy Bagmati River. Witness spiritual rituals, holy Sadhus, and golden pagoda roofs.

Holy Bagmati Ghats Sacred Rituals
04:00 PM Step 06

Boudhanath Stupa & Return Drop-Off

Walk around one of the world's largest spherical stupas, listening to Tibetan monks chanting. Conclude your day with a comfortable private drop-off at your hotel.

Hotel drop-off included

Helpful Travel Tips

A few simple tips will help you make the most of your Kathmandu day tour.

Dress Comfortably

Wear comfortable shoes and respectful clothing when visiting religious sites.

Protect Yourself From the Sun

Carry sunglasses, sunscreen and a water bottle.

Bring Your Camera

Kathmandu offers countless opportunities for photography.
