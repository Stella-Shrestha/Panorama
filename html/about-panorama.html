About Panorama Trekking

It is because each person is different that the journey will be unique. From this desire for exchange and sharing was born Panorama Himalaya Trekking, a French-speaking trekking agency in Nepal.

Discover Our Story Contact Us

20+

Years of significant experience

Accompanying and organizing trekking expeditions throughout Nepal.

Your local French-speaking agency

Originally, two people, Kusang and his brother Pasang, started creating a French-speaking trekking agency in Nepal in 2010. Originally from the mountains and belonging to the Sherpa people who once guided travellers on their mountain routes. They decided to pass on their love of travel. Having started as porters, guides and then travel tour organizers, they now have 20 years’ significant experience in accompanying and organizing trekking expeditions.

The passion for the mountains and the profession of guide was passed down from father to son:

“ When we were children, we saw Dad working in tourism and we thought it was possible to live from his passion. “

Mission and vision

We create journeys that respect each traveler, support local communities and celebrate the natural and cultural richness of Nepal.

Our mission

To provide deeply immersive trekking experiences that honor the earth and empower local communities. We bridge the gap between wilderness exploration and responsible travel, ensuring that every footprint we leave behind contributes positively to the preservation of fragile ecosystems and the economic well-being of mountain families.

Our vision

To pioneer a sustainable future for adventure travel where tourism acts as a direct force for conservation. We aim to inspire a global community of conscious travelers who respect local traditions, protect natural habitats, and ensure the majestic trails we love today remain pristine for generations to come.

The services offered

As the meetings and exchanges progressed, the small agency began to evolve and now has 10 specialized guides. Panorama Himalaya offers trekking and high mountain experiences adapted to your desires and physical conditions in Nepalese regions such as Mustang, Everest, Annapurnas, Kanchenjunga, Mount Kailash… We also offer other activities such as cultural visits to Nepal’s cities and sports activities such as rafting. To make everyone feel comfortable on their trip, our guides are French-speaking. English, Italian and Spanish are also commonly used.

Education support

We contribute to projects that improve access to education for children and rural families.

Because each person can contribute to a beautiful project!

“The mountain is part of our lives, just like its inhabitants. The founders and guides of this association are keen to contribute to the education of the local population. In Nepal, schooling remains expensive for rural families, which is why Panorama Himalaya Trekking invests in access to education and donates part of the profits to the association Soutienenterrenépalaise, which specializes in education. Other associations are involved in building schools and improving facilities such as Nepaletvous. By choosing our trekking agency in Nepal you will contribute to developing the country’s educational conditions.
