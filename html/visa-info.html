Nepal tourist visa guide.

There are different ways to obtain a Nepalese visa: on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, at designated land border points, through a Nepalese embassy or consulate, or through the official online application system before travel.

Visa-on-arrival counters are available at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu.

Where to obtain your visa.

A visa can be obtained on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, at Nepal-India and Nepal-China border entry points, or from Nepalese embassies and consulates near you. Travelers can also complete a pre-application online before reaching Nepal.

Online pre-applications must be submitted within two weeks before arrival. If the application is completed too early, it expires and has to be submitted again.

Airport Arrival

Visa-on-arrival counters are available at Kathmandu's international airport.

Land Borders

Tourist visas can be issued at designated Nepal-India and Nepal-China entry points.

Embassy or Consulate

Apply before travel through a Nepalese diplomatic mission in your country.

Online Form

Submit your form online and keep the receipt with barcode for immigration.

Tourist visa fees.

Visa fees on arrival are organised by stay length. For any period longer than 30 days, choose the 90-day tourist visa.

Fees below apply at ports of entry and are shown in US dollars.

Arrival process organised by step.

The visa-on-arrival process is simple when your form, payment receipt, passport and photo are ready.

01

Step One

Fill in the arrival card

Complete the tourist visa form before arrival or at the airport.

Fill in the tourist visa form online before arrival, or use the kiosk machines at Tribhuvan International Airport. If you complete it online, print the receipt with barcode and bring it with you.

The online form works for 15 days. After that, it becomes invalid and must be completed again.

Make the payment at the bank counter according to your visa duration: 15, 30 or 90 days. Keep the receipt for the immigration desk.

Different payment methods may be accepted, but cash in major currencies usually keeps the process moving faster.

Visa extensions for longer mountain journeys

Need more time in Nepal? Tourist visa extensions are handled at Department of Immigration offices in Kathmandu and Pokhara. You will need a valid passport and a passport-size photo for the extension.

  • Minimum extension: $45 for the first 15 days.
  • Additional extension days: $3 per day.
  • Maximum tourist stay: 150 days per visa year.
